GARASU

Glass Aging, Relaxation And Simulation UtilityBeta

Every glass carries the thermal fingerprint of how it was made, written in its fictive temperature. GARASU simulates and fits that history, using the heterogeneous Takeda–Lucas model alongside TNM and RelaxPy, for DSC, MDSC, enthalpy, volume and annealing data.

Data AnalysisYour data

Turn raw DSC heat flow and relaxation data into normalized curves, fictive temperatures and enthalpy.

  • Glass and liquid baselines → CpN, Tf(T), Tf′ for heating and cooling scans
  • Heating after annealing: aligned scans, ΔHF, recovered ΔH(ta), φ and a KWW fit
  • Volume / density / length during annealing: stretched-exponential fit

Models

Takeda–Lucas (TL)Heterogeneous Adam–Gibbs domains with a distribution of Tv; temperature-dependent nonexponentiality and enthalpy fluctuation.
Tool–Narayanaswamy–MoynihanSingle fictive temperature with nonlinearity x and a KWW kernel (exact summation or Prony series).
RelaxPy (MAP)Mauro–Allan–Potuzak nonequilibrium viscosity with Prony-series fictive-temperature components.
