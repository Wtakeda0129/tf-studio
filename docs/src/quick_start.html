2 · Good to know

  • Nothing runs until you click. In the Fitter the model runs only on Compute or Run fit; any change clears the old result.
  • Temperature K / °C and Time axis (top right) apply everywhere; K/°C also converts inputs and Tg. Time axis auto shows real time whenever the temperature changes (scans), log time for isothermal holds.
  • Try before your own data: every tool has examples (Fitter Examples ▾, Data Analysis Load example data).
  • Projects: New project in the header keeps normalized scans, fits and sessions of one study together; Save to project in Data Analysis and in the Fitter results.
  • Figures: Export ▾ on every plot saves PNG, JPEG, SVG or CSV.
  • Private: everything runs on your computer; no data are uploaded.
  • Help: see the User Guide, or report issues at github.com/wtakeda-research/GARASU/issues.

3 · Three things to try

≈ 2 min

A · Simulate a DSC scan after aging (Fitter)

  1. Open Fitter. In step 1 Thermal history, choose the template Aging below Tg, then DSC heating.
  2. Step 3 Model & parameters: keep Takeda–Lucas or pick a Preset (e.g. Selenium).
  3. Step 4 Compute & fit: click Compute. You get Tf, normalized Cp, relaxation during the hold, τ and, for TL, the map of local fictive temperatures.
≈ 3 min

B · Fit a model to data (Fitter)

  1. Examples ▾ → Aging + DSC with synthetic enthalpy data (or add your own data in step 2 with + Add dataset, linked to the matching segment).
  2. Step 3: tick the parameters to fit and check their min/max.
  3. Step 4: open Fit parameters, choose Local (fast) or Global (thorough), click Run fit. Read the values ± errors, R², and the correlation matrix.
≈ 3 min

C · Analyze raw DSC heat flow (Data Analysis)

  1. Open Data Analysis → + Add run. Load your file or paste columns; check temperature and heat-flow columns, units, the sign convention (endo up/down), rate and mass. For scans after annealing, enter ta and Ta.
  2. Set the glass range and liquid range (or Auto ranges). Read Tf′, the midpoint and ΔCp; Apply to all runs.
  3. Tab 2 Enthalpy recovery: choose the unaged reference and enter Ta to get ΔH, Tf and φ(ta) with a KWW fit. Tab 3 fits volume (density, length…) during annealing.
